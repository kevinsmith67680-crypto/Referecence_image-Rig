# Phase 3.3 report: clothing distortion prevention and garment rigging

**Verdict: PARTIAL.** The causes of the distortion found in the audit are fixed or now reported: rubber bands,
puffy shoulders, layer-cut seam cracks, and generated pixels covering original artwork. The structured garment rig
renders the real schoolboy with rigid garment segments, narrow joint patches, aligned shoulder pivots and
hidden pixels drawn under the right layer.

It is PARTIAL because:
- No person has reviewed the renders. The garment evaluator still reports FAIL or NEEDS_ARTWORK somewhere in every
  test pose.
- Several drawings are missing: armpit gussets, deep elbow, bent knee, seated thighs, a 3/4 torso, a raised-shoulder
  collar, and the shoe's top under the trouser hem.
- Moho did not run.

Mesh weighting code alone is not a PASS. Visual quality is judged on the renders in
`projects/schoolboy_tpose/character_package/natural_pose/garments/` and has not been approved by anyone.

## 1. Root causes

They are confirmed in `docs/phase3_3_clothing_audit.md` with the renders at commit 66f6730:

- **R1:** wide rotation-interpolation bands, widened further with the joint angle. They made rubber elbows and knees,
  and folds. 82 % of the sleeve was blended.
- **R2:** the shoulder pivot sat on the sleeve's mid-axis. The sleeve cap swung outward, giving puffy shoulders
  386 px wide.
- **R3:** garment semantics were ignored. The lapels and shoulder pads were pulled by the arm.
- **R4:** each layer was warped on its own along straight Phase 1 cuts, which cracked the seams.
- **R5:** generated pixels were drawn at their own layer's depth. The trouser extensions covered the shoes
  (about 1,400 px per shoe).
- **R6:** artwork was missing.

The solver, the IK and the hand switches were not causes.

## 2. Pivots

`garmentrig.structure.align_pivots` moves each shoulder pivot (the upper-arm head, with the clavicle tail following it)
0.55 h toward the top of the armhole: 30.0 px on the right, 28.3 px on the left. So a lowered sleeve hangs from the
shoulder point. Before, it rotated about the mid-axis and its cap swung out.

- **Bones the user edited are kept.** Bones placed in the editor (`method == "editor"`) keep their position.
- **Anatomical axis.** The upper arm keeps its anatomical axis (old joint → elbow) as `Bone.axis_offset`
  (13.1° / −12.8°). Abduction, flexion, the shoulder rhythm, FK, the correction readings and `fk_params` use the axis.
  The IK geometry uses the pivot line.
- **IK limits per bend side.** `ik.solve_axis_aware` shifts the IK's flexion limits per bend side, so they apply to
  the anatomical elbow. A straight IK line is never a reverse-bent elbow.
- **FK↔IK switching** stays exact (≤ 0.05 px), including the hands-on-hips shoulder compensation, which is now
  carried by `fk.clavicle_extra`.

**Validation** (`validate_pivots`, in `structure.json`) checks every limb pivot:
- inside the silhouette;
- its position across the limb's own layers;
- not at a layer's bounding-box centre.

Results:
- Elbows, wrists, hips and knees: ok.
- Shoulders: ok. They are off-centre by design: cross-section fraction 0.65 / 0.53, toward the top contour.
- Ankles: **warning**. Cross-section 0.51 / 0.45 across shin + shoe; the ankle sits high in the shoe. Review it in the
  Joints tab.

**Side effects, by design and documented:**
- **Hands hang higher.** The hanging arms now hang from the shoulder point, so the hands sit about 30 px higher. That
  is faithful to the drawn sleeve length.
- **Hands-on-hips target.** The preset now keeps its target within 90 % of the arm's reach
  (`CharacterProfile.shoulder`).
- **Walk arc.** The elbow arc of the walk was raised from 4° to 8°, so the fluid hand loop stays larger than the
  plain cycle's (quotient 0.0635 vs 0.0563).
- **Grip IK.** It now holds the arm's current bend side. Near a hanging arm, the default pole was collinear with the
  arm and could flip the elbow.

**Pivot editor:**
- The Weights tab lists the aligned pivots.
- Pivots are moved with the existing Joints tab; an edited bone keeps its position.
- The diagnostic overlay `structure_and_pivots.png` shows the old and new shoulder pivots.

## 3. Mesh weighting

`GarmentStructureAnalyzer` labels every layer pixel with a garment part: 41 parts, 13 RIGID, 12 LIMITED_DEFORMATION
and 16 FLEXIBLE. These cover:

- **Blazer:** body, shoulders, sleeve cap, upper and forearm sleeve, elbow fold, lapels, badge, 2 buttons, 2 pocket
  flaps, hem.
- **Shirt and tie:** collar, cuffs, tie.
- **Sweater.**
- **Trousers:** waistband, hip fold, thigh, knee fold, shin, cuff.
- **Shoes.**
- **Body:** hands, neck, head, hair.

How the labels are made:
- Labels come from colour, the layer masks and joint planes, not from the Phase 1 cuts. The blazer hem lives in the
  pelvis layer and in both thigh layers; finger fragments cut into the forearm layer follow the hand.
- Each part lists its attachment points, fold regions and allowed bones (`structure.json`).
- There are 22 attachments: shoulder seam, armpit, elbow (both sides), cuff, wrist, hip, knee (both sides), ankle,
  collar and waist.

`WeightSet` builds a 6 px grid mesh per layer (53 meshes, 29,230 active vertices).

**Rules on every vertex:**
- one weight per allowed bone;
- each weight ≥ 0, and the weights sum to 1;
- zero outside the vertex part's bone restriction;
- RIGID parts are locked.

Pixels are bilinear in the grid, so they stay normalised. A bone's influence is the weight of its subtree. The
automatic weights are part- and joint-plane-based, not distance-based.

**Transitions** (in units of the joint's half-thickness h):

| Joint | Band | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shoulder | [0, 0.5] | sleeve side only |
| Elbow | [−0.15, 0.45] | |
| Knee | [−0.25, 0.35] | |
| Ankle | [−0.6, 0.45] | the trouser hem rests on the shoe |
| Spine / chest | ±0.9 / ±0.8 | LIMITED torso |
| Hip | Phase 3.1 soft attachment | under the hem, crotch logic |

The LIMITED torso parts are smoothed together (Gaussian, σ = 2.5 vertices), so the lapel and collar never shear
against the shoulder.

**Edits.** The user edits weights with operations:
- `weights.paint`, `erase`, `smooth`, `normalise`, `lock` and `reset`;
- they are stored in the editor project (schema v5, migration from v4) and replayed deterministically;
- they never touch the source image.

`WeightSet.explain` says why a point follows its bones: the part, the class, the allowed bones, the transition values,
the weights, locks and edits. `autorig garment-weights <pkg> --explain LAYER X Y` and `--export` expose this on the
command line.

**Performance:**

| | Structured | Legacy |
|---|---|---|
| Engine build | 9.5 s | 4.9 s |
| Pose (mean) | 2.98 s (max 4.00 s) | 2.20 s |
| Garment evaluation (included) | 0.45 s | — |

Measured on the 4-core cloud sandbox, not on the user's M1.

## 4. Constraints

The structured deformer (`garmentrig.deform.StructuredDeformer`) keeps the Phase 2 angle interpolation; it has no
axial scale and no cloth simulation. It adds:

- **Rigid segments.** The upper sleeve, the forearm sleeve with its cuff, and the trouser shin are drawn with their
  bone's exact world transform over the weighted joint patch. Inner sides overlap, and the patch shows only in the
  wedge that opens.
- **Rigid fits for decorations.** The badge, buttons, tie and pocket flaps are drawn with the rigid fit
  (rotation + translation) of the local deformation. Their residual is graded 0 px (PASS) in every test.
- **Stretch limit (conflict reporting).** Joint-patch pixels stretched beyond `max_patch_stretch` are not drawn:
  4× in general, 1.8× on the underarm side of the shoulder. Neither are pixels where the inverse map only converged
  on alternate rows (stripes). The gap that remains is counted per garment part (`render.clipped_uncovered`), and the
  evaluator reports it with the corrective asset it needs. Fabric is never smeared silently. This fixed the striped
  armpit smear of the both-arms-raised pose.
- **Exact rigidity where required.** Locked rigid vertices, gradual LIMITED transitions, and cuffs on the forearm only.

Edge-length preservation, inversions, area change, silhouette (rigid-fit residual) and attachments are measured by
the evaluator (section 10), not enforced by an optimiser. ARAP was not used: the rigid segments already preserve
shape exactly, and the remaining deformation is local to the joint patches.

## 5. Shoulders and sleeves

**What improved:**
- The sleeve hangs from the shoulder point.
- The chest silhouette and lapels no longer take any arm rotation (lapels and blazer body cannot follow an arm bone).
- The shoulder fan creases are gone: the band covers half a thickness on the sleeve side, not 0.85 h + 1 h across the
  seam.
- The cap is rounded by the patch with its outline intact.
- The cuffs stay on the forearm: 95th-percentile cuff displacement from the forearm transform is 0 px in every test,
  including stored hand switches.
- The sleeve length is unchanged (rigid segments).

**Shoulder sweep** (0/30/60/90/120°): SUPPORTED with clean sleeves up to 90°. At 120° the underarm wedge opens and
`gusset` is required (pending-review procedural gussets exist and are only previewed with `allow_pending`).

**Elbow sweep:**

| Bend | Result |
|---|---|
| 0° | Clean |
| 45° | Clean, rounded outer elbow (`elbow_45_95_135_after.png`) |
| 90–95° | Cut-out elbow; the outer wedge fill has a ragged lower edge; `sleeve_crease` drawing optional |
| 135° | NEEDS_ARTWORK (`elbow_deep`, required above 125°, read on the anatomical axis) |

The both-arms-raised pose reports `gusset` and `raised_shoulder_collar_{r,l}`. With a 26° shoulder elevation the lapel
tips and collar wings compress next to the sternoclavicular pivot.

## 6. Blazer body

The chest, lapels, collar, sweater and tie are LIMITED or RIGID parts, weighted only to the torso bones and the
shoulder girdle. The shoulder share fades below the shoulder line.

- The badge, buttons, pocket flaps and tie are rigid.
- The hem hangs from the pelvis: no thigh influence, and the Phase 3.1 hem lag is kept as a weight-mesh field.
- The spine/chest bends are spread over the wide LIMITED bands.

The evaluator flags the lapels and collar at a 99th-percentile stretch of 0.50–0.58 in the neutral pose. The neutral
close-up (`closeups/01_neutral.jpg`) shows no visible lapel or collar distortion, so this is a sensitive percentile on
a few pixels at the collar points. It is reported, not hidden. A person must judge it.

A hanging sleeve now overlaps the chest edge with a straight diagonal: the sleeve's T-pose underside drawn as the inner
edge. This is visible in the close-ups.

## 7. Trousers

- **Waistband:** stays on the pelvis.
- **Thighs:** keep the Phase 3.1 soft attachment under the hem.
- **Knees:** bend as a rigid shin over a narrow patch. The squat's balloon knees are gone. The knee now reads as an
  angle with a small light fringe at the inner corner, and `knee_deep` is reported above 45°.
- **Shoes:** never deformed (rigid on the foot). The trouser extension hidden under the shoe is drawn under the shoe
  (R5 fixed), while the hem's original pixels stay over it.
- **Hem on the shoe.** The hem follows the shoe across the ankle band (`hem_follows_foot = 1.0`), because a partial
  follow lifted a hem corner off the shoe. Where the shoe turns against the shin, a 1–2 px sliver (up to a wedge when
  squatting) still opens at the hem corner: the shoe's top under the hem was never drawn. It is reported as
  `corrective:foot_extension_under_hem_{r,l}` (hem-on-shoe trigger, on above 6°) in every standing pose.
- **Sitting and running knee drive:** NEEDS_ARTWORK (`trousers_seated`, `hip_flex`, `knee_drive`).

## 8. Correctives

`GarmentCorrectionController` extends the Phase 3.2 `CorrectionSwitchController` (same triggers, approvals and
hysteresis; no second switch engine).

**Each trigger stores:**
- `angle_range` (continuous_max, full);
- `method` (blend or switch);
- `attachment` (bone and pivot);
- `layer_order`;
- `approval`;
- `missing_dependencies`;
- `handling` (continuous deformation, drawing, or fallback);
- an eased `target_blend` (smoothstep over the range, monotonic: no popping).

**Triggers:**
- the Phase 3.2 ones (shoulder raise, deep elbow, sleeve crease, wrist rotation, hip flexion, deep knee, trouser fold);
- `shoulder_lift_collar` and `hem_on_shoe` per side;
- the pose-level `torso_lean_sagittal`, `sitting_trousers` and `running_knee_drive`.

`blend_weight` stays 0 while the drawing is missing or unapproved. Nothing is approved in code. The garment evaluator
turns a failing part covered by such a correction into NEEDS_ARTWORK with the asset named.

## 9. Weight editor

The editor's **Weights** tab (`editor/src/components/WeightsPanel.tsx`) offers:
- **Overlays:** the garment-part overlay, a bone-influence heatmap and the mesh topology (active, locked and edited
  vertices).
- **Brushes:** paint, erase, smooth, lock and unlock on the canvas, with radius and strength, and an optional
  part-restriction zone.
- **Buttons:** normalise layer, reset layer edits.
- **Comparison:** auto vs edited weights side by side, for the current preset.
- **Explain:** "explain" mode shows why a clicked point follows its bones.
- **Pivots:** the pivot list.

Edits are project operations, with undo/redo, versions and save/reload. They update the cached engine incrementally,
with no rebuild. The source image is never written.

Endpoints: `GET /api/garments`, `GET /api/garments/overlay.png`, `POST /api/garments/explain`,
`GET /api/garments/compare.png`. The Playwright e2e step 6f covers the panel, a brush op (project op), explain, undo
and the validation, with screenshots in `docs/phase3_3/screenshots/`.

## 14. Live puppeteering

The structured rig is deterministic:
- the weights replay from operations, with no randomness;
- there is no cloud inference in posing;
- repeated updates reuse the cached engine.

Correctives have hysteresis and eased blend weights, so they do not pop or jitter. The face and head are RIGID on the
head bone and never move with the garments. The gamepad and live action map is unchanged (`rig_controls.json`,
SHOWTIME mappings `proposed_unverified`). There is no webcam tracking.

A 3 s CPU render per pose is not real-time. Live Mode in Moho would use Moho's native deformation after the Phase 4
conversion. Whether that converted rig runs efficiently on the user's M1 is unverified.
